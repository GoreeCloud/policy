# Privacy Policy

The current Development reference runtime does not persist policy requests or decisions and does not implement analytics or external telemetry.

Policy context should contain only attributes required to make the requested decision. Callers are responsible for minimizing personal and sensitive information according to Privacy Shield and applicable governance.

Health/readiness responses contain component and development-state information only. Future telemetry, persistence, distribution, or administrative features require separate privacy review and updated documentation before production acceptance.
