# Planned Features

All entries remain unimplemented or unaccepted unless later moved to `IMPLEMENTED-FEATURES.md` with repository evidence.

- **POL-IDENTITY-001 — GoreeCloud Identity integration:** authenticate services/actors and preserve authoritative identity context without making Policy the identity owner.
- **POL-MESH-001 — Mesh distribution:** versioned policy bundle and decision transport with provenance and replay protection.
- **POL-MANAGER-001 — Manager administration:** bounded administrative policy lifecycle APIs and approval surfaces.
- **POL-PRIVACY-001 — Privacy Shield adapter:** evaluate privacy-owned rules without redefining privacy semantics.
- **POL-WARDVEIL-001 — Wardveil adapter:** evaluate security-owned rules without redefining security semantics.
- **POL-EVERKEEP-001 — Continuity:** durable policy/configuration backup, restore, export, and recovery acceptance.
- **POL-OBS-001 — Observability:** privacy-safe health, latency, decision-result, and failure evidence with freshness/provenance.
- **POL-DISTRIBUTION-001 — Signed policy bundles:** durable versioning, validation, signature verification, rollback, and revocation.
- **POL-ENFORCEMENT-001 — Enforcement adapters:** explicit enforcement receipts preserving decision identity and result.
- **POL-STABLE-001 — Stable qualification:** exact-release tests, runtime acceptance, recovery, security/privacy review, deployment/rollback, and production evidence.
